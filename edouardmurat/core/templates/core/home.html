About Me

I build gameplay systems, and I lead the people who build them.

For the past year I led a five-person programming team at Sigma Digital, building curriculum-aligned literacy games delivered on Android tablets to first- and second-grade children in Africa. I hired the team, owned the architecture, and ran code review and sprint planning.

Before games I spent six years as a professional developer — five of them writing production C# at Fujitsu Consulting, on a payment solution for HydroSherbrooke and the daycare management system for the Ministère de la Famille du Québec — and later helped lead a development team at BMC Software, where I also interviewed candidates.

I also hold a psychology degree, and spent seven years facilitating workshops and training managers and facilitators. That sounds like a detour and it isn't: it's why I think carefully about onboarding, difficulty curves, and what a playtest is actually telling you.

Day to day: Godot and Unity, GDScript and C#, gameplay systems, AI behavior, procedural generation, and shaders. Everything below is playable.
