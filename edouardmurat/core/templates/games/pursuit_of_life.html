Pursuit of Life Life Sim

A pixel-art urban life-sim: ride the bus, take the subway, and move between the apartment, the clothing store, and the music store.

Built around a dynamic weather system: real-time rain, per-tile wetness tracking, and puddle reflections for the player, NPCs, and foliage alike.

Technical Highlights

  • • Dynamic rain system with customizable impact zones and per-region wetness tracking
  • • Real-time puddle reflections for sprites, NPCs, and swaying foliage
  • • Day/night lighting driven by a global time-of-day system
  • • Handcrafted city scenes: apartment, bus, subway platform and train, clothing store, music store
