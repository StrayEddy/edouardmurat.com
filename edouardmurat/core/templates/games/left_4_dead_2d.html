Left 4 Dead 2D Fan Game

A 2D fan-made tribute to the iconic zombie survival series.

Choose your favorite character and survive wave after wave of the infected. Work together with allies in this pixelated side-scroller to stay alive and make it to the next safe room.

Removed from Google Play due to copyright restrictions. It was a fan project made for learning purposes.

  • • Mobile-friendly optimization with horde-based enemy encounters
